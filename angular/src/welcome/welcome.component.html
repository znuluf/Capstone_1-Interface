What FPMS keeps track of

Everything the paper logbook and the spreadsheet used to hold, in one record you can trace.

Feeding

Morning and evening feeds go on one sheet per pond, and feed stock draws down as you log.

Water quality

Readings are checked against the GAqP ranges as you type, with a nudge when one drifts.

Growth and survival

Days of culture, average weight, FCR and survival are worked out for you.

Harvest and sales

Record harvests by size grade, then sales to buyers and the delivery costs that go with them.

Permits and licenses

Soonest-expiring first, with in-app reminders before anything lapses.

Reports

Named reports with the summary on top and a CSV export when you need the numbers elsewhere.

Built for wet hands

Most entries happen at the pond edge, on a shared tablet, usually in a hurry.

  • Big buttons and fields sized for fingers, not mice.
  • Interrupted? Your unsaved log is kept for you and nobody else.
  • Handing over the tablet? Switch user takes a moment.
  • Works on the farm's local network, with no Internet needed.
